# Codex contribution

Codex contributed to the product design and implementation of the independent,
synthetic-only prototype.

The contribution includes the bilingual Kannada and English guided citizen
journey; deterministic fictional records, source evidence, aliases, and
fixtures; the judge-only synthetic data explorer; self-hosted hybrid retrieval
and deterministic field-aware result states; the read-only API; and accessible
keyboard, focus, language, narrow-screen, and source-verification interactions.

Codex also helped establish the privacy boundaries: possible matches are not
official decisions, real-person browse-all access is outside the product
design, citizen queries do not use runtime LLM, web-search, third-party
embedding, analytics, OCR, indexing, upload, or live-government surfaces, and
future personal or restricted records remain with the responsible authority.

The work includes unit, integration, browser, performance, and security
verification of the fictional citizen journey, synthetic catalog, source
evidence, query-free URL behavior, storage and cookie boundaries, and the
deployment smoke contract.

All records, source pages, crops, aliases, and examples in this prototype are
fictional. OCR remains outside the citizen search path until it meets the
required accuracy bar; OCR output does not supply demo search results.
