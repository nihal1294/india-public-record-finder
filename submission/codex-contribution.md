# Codex contribution

Codex materially planned, implemented, tested, reviewed, and privacy-audited
the prototype.

The contribution covered the synthetic-only product boundary; deterministic
source and record fixtures; manifest integrity and evidence-crop handling;
in-service lexical and multilingual retrieval; deterministic field-aware ranking;
the read-only API; the guided browser journey; and focused unit, integration,
browser, performance, and security checks. Codex also reviewed the candidate
states, source-verification flow, no-query-egress boundary, no-store responses,
and the absence of runtime OCR, ingestion, indexing, upload, and remote-model
surfaces.

All examples and source records in the prototype are fictional. OCR is outside
the citizen search path until it meets the required accuracy bar; no OCR output
supplies demo search results.
