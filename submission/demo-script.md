# Two-minute demonstration script

The timed visual direction is outside the spoken-word count. Use only fictional
examples and keep captions identical to the locked narration. The production
must remain at or below 02:00.

## Locked narration

[00:00-00:07] Find a record. Verify source. Synthetic Karnataka prototype.

[00:07-00:18] Historical rolls are hard across scripts, spellings, relatives,
and localities.

[00:18-00:31] A reviewer opens Browse fictional demo data. This judge-only
bench contains fictional records. Use this sample pre-fills; it waits for Find
possible matches. Browse-all for real people violates the privacy design.

[00:31-00:43] We submit. Kannada and English aliases, typo-tolerant matching,
and pre-indexed multilingual retrieval surface possible matches. Deterministic
scoring sets result state. No runtime query goes to an LLM, web search, or
third-party embedding provider.

[00:43-00:54] If needed, add relative, locality, or age. Check synthetic source
crop, part, and page. A possible match is not an official result.

[00:54-01:00] No confident match avoids pretending uncertainty
is an answer.

[01:00-01:12] Document discovery spans fragmented authorities, identifiers,
scripts, and access rules. One question should not require expertise in every
portal.

[01:12-01:24] Structured public PDF paths can lower automation and correlation
barriers. Not an allegation of illegality. Individual inspection and
machine-scale discovery need safeguards.

[01:24-01:37] This approach keeps source visible and guides one purpose, not a
people database. Its interaction design was informed by private family
research; no family data or real electoral-roll record is included.

[01:37-01:51] The pattern could guide public-source navigation for gazettes,
RERA, environmental approvals, archives, and reference-led courts. It could
also guide authority-managed retrieval for certificates, benefits, municipal
services, education, and RTI.

[01:51-02:00] Land/property mixes public metadata and authority-managed
workflows. A future adapter navigates jurisdiction and identifiers, never an
owner-name index or title decision. Only synthetic Karnataka SIR works today.
